Formulas

  • ROMI = (attributed revenue - spend) / spend
  • MER = total revenue / spend
  • CAC = spend / new customers
  • LTV = average order value x gross margin x lifespan (in orders)

A common rule of thumb is an LTV:CAC ratio of 3 or higher for a healthy growth model.
